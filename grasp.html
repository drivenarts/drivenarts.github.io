GRASP: Fast Food Training

VR training for Dunkin’ Donuts employees built in Unreal Engine.

Watch Demo Request Build
Prototype / Early Demo · Team Size: 5 · United States

Elevator Pitch

GRASP is an Unreal Engine VR training app that lets new Dunkin' hires master peak-hour operations in 15 minutes.

Why now: This is a $17.5B annual problem we can attack with a $299 headset and very modest development costs [2].

Overview

  • Fast food is a $1.1 trillion industry with 13 million employees [1].
  • $17.5 billion yearly cost associated with high employee turnover and retraining [2].
  • VR training reduces training time and cuts costs across industries [3] [4] [5] [6] [7].
  • Headsets are now at an accessible price point ($299) and getting cheaper.

Problem

  • Existing training solutions are dangerously bad. We took the online crew member training for Dunkin’ and were not prepared for the job - pictures and text on a website do not work.
  • High turnover rates and ineffective training increase operational costs and reduce workforce efficiency.
  • Restaurant owners are out of touch with the next generation of crew members they need to hire and train.

Solution

  • Hands‑on experience in a real virtual store for fun, immersive, risk‑free, interactive learning.
  • A modular Unreal Engine training platform simulating peak store operations and multiple roles.
  • Meets the next generation of gamers in a way they enjoy.
  • “A spoonful of sugar makes the medicine go down.”

Does It Work?

  • Yes - companies like OssoVR report up to 300% improvements in outcomes for surgical training [8].
  • Early testers of our Dunkin’ VR training show a remarkable increase in competence.
  • A Dunkin’ franchise owner told us he learned more about a crew members role from our demo than from official corporate materials.

What We’ve Built

  • Prototype of beverage training in a virtual Dunkin' store.
    • Small hot coffee (10 steps)
    • Medium cappuccino (15 steps)
    • Signature latte (20 steps)

Early validation: franchise owners found our demo more informative than official corporate training.

Strategy & Roadmap

  • We are approaching Inspire Brands (owners of Dunkin’) with a $100,000 pilot proposal.
  • With Epic’s support, we can deliver a V1 pilot that is impossible to refuse.
  • Each milestone is tied to clear deliverables and funding sources.

Milestone 1 (Epic MegaGrant – $25,000, 6 months)

Objective: Deliver a polished 3‑module pilot (Beverage + Sandwich Station + Drive‑Thru) to Inspire Brands.

Total: $25,000 | 500 hrs @ $50/hr

  • A) Beverage Station — completion – $6,000 (120 hrs)
    • Completed: Coffee (easy), Cappuccino (medium), Signature Caramel Latte (hard).
    • Remaining 20 beverages:
      • Espresso & Coffee: Iced Coffee, Cold Brew, Americano, Iced Americano, Latte, Iced Latte, Macchiato, Iced Macchiato, Espresso
      • Teas: Tea, Iced Tea, Hot Chocolate, Matcha Latte, Iced Matcha Latte, Chai Latte, Iced Chai Latte
      • Frozen: Frozen Coffee, Coolatta, Frozen Chocolate, Frozen Matcha Latte
  • B) Sandwich Station – $9,000 (180 hrs)
    • Art complete; implement guided assembly gameplay for:
      • Sausage, Egg & Cheese; Bacon, Egg & Cheese; Turkey Sausage, Egg & Cheese; Egg & Cheese
      • Sourdough Breakfast Sandwich; Wake‑up Wrap; Hash Browns; Snackin’ Bacon
      • Stuffed Bagel Minis; Bagels with Cream Cheese; Muffins; Croissant; English Muffin
  • C) Drive‑Thru – $10,000 (200 hrs)
    • Implement remaining POS menus (bakery, sandwiches, retail, local, functions) and develop customer interactions that challenge the player across menus.

Milestone 2 (Inspire Brands Pilot – $100,000, ~12 months)

  • A funded pilot across approximately 10 Dunkin’ stores.
  • Complete the Donut Prep module (batter, cutting, frying, glazing/decorating).
  • Implement the Expediter role (order review, accuracy/error detection).
  • Deliver a full V1 training experience simulating 15 minutes of peak operations.
  • Deploy and evaluate in ~10 Dunkin’ pilot locations; capture performance data and trainee outcomes.

Milestone 3 (Dunkin’ Rollout – $2–3M, 18–24 months)

  • If/when the pilot succeeds, Inspire Brands could fund a full rollout across Dunkin’s ~9,500 locations.
  • National deployment for Dunkin’.
  • Enterprise hardening: device fleet management, analytics dashboards, compliance reporting.
  • Train thousands of new employees annually with Unreal‑based VR modules.
  • Establish Unreal Engine as the enterprise training backbone for Dunkin’.

Milestone 4 (Multi‑brand Expansion & SDK – $10M+, multi‑year)

  • After Dunkin’ success, Inspire will extend training to all brands (Arby’s, Sonic, Jimmy John’s, Baskin‑Robbins, Buffalo Wild Wings).

  • Release/extend the open XR SDK/template for third‑party Unreal developers.
